on LLM systems: agent memory, decoding, and interpretability. Three of that work's outputs
are public preprints; two more are under review and are not discussed here beyond their
titles.

## What connects them

Each one turns a soft claim into something checkable. A hosted provider's "you are talking
to the model you paid for" becomes a commitment opened against a Merkle root. An agent's
"I remember what we discussed" becomes a measured recall rate against an evicted page. An
editing model's "this file is mostly unchanged" becomes a grammar in which copying is a
first-class token and the copy is verified by construction rather than sampled and hoped for.
